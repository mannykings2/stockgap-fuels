The company

A Port Harcourt terminal built to shorten the LPG journey.

Stockgap Fuels Limited was incorporated in Nigeria as a private limited liability company and is engaged in the distribution of refined petroleum products and Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG), sourced directly from international trading partners or through coastal lifting under a strategic alliance with the NNPC.

The company’s LPG terminal at Rumuolumeni was commissioned in 2019, ending the practice of shipping product to Lagos and trucking it back to the Niger Delta. Product now discharges directly at the Port Harcourt jetty for onward distribution to distributors, retailers and households in the region.

Safety, compliance & sustainability

LPG is only useful if it is handled without incident.

Storage and distribution of a flammable product depend on discipline: controlled access, documented inspection, trained people and records that survive an audit.

01
Operational safety

Permit-controlled work, documented walk-downs and recorded handovers at every transfer point.

02
Incident and hazard reporting

HSE observations, corrective actions and closure tracked with the same rigour as commercial records.

03
Environmental responsibility

LPG distribution displaces kerosene and firewood use. Claims about emissions or savings require measurement and are not made here.

04
Regulatory alignment

The platform is designed to record the licences, inspections and certificates that NMDPRA, SON and NUPRC requirements demand. It does not itself confer compliance.
