# Company research and sources

The corporate website reproduces public reporting about Stockgap Fuels Limited and labels
third-party figures as reported. Nothing here is company disclosure, and no capacity,
expansion or offtake claim should be quoted as official without checking the source.

## Claims deliberately not made on the website

- No current storage capacity is asserted as fact or as a single number. Every page that
  states capacity uses the same range (8,000 – 15,000 MT) and explains that it is two
  attributed reports: 8,000 MT around commissioning and 15,000 MT in 2022 reporting.
- No capacity figure is quoted without its source and its date.
- No pricing, volume availability, credit terms or delivery commitments.
- No claim that the Lagos, Abuja or retail locations are company facilities: those exist
  only inside the prototype and are labelled as demonstration locations.
- No staff names, customer names or contracts from the demonstration data are presented as
  real relationships.

## Photography

The images in `web/public/images/` were supplied for this build. They are used as
illustrative site photography, not as documented events or identified individuals.

## Demonstration data notice

Every record inside the portal — orders, stock levels, counterparties, prices, staff,
incidents and enquiries — is sample data created to exercise the software. The demo
accounts and the seeded business names are fictional.
